原讲义仅作为语境、词汇和语法锚点；练习时继续采用“原课语境 -> 核心表达 -> 语法与词汇锚点 -> 现实语境扩展 -> 角色扮演”的结构。资料整理不等于掌握，只有独立练习后才更新掌握状态。

## A2 第一批：家庭、人物、城市、作息、频率、宠物与健康

### A. 兄弟姐妹、年龄与身高

对应主题：1. My brothers

核心表达：
- How many brothers do you have?
- I have one big brother and one little brother.
- I'm in the middle.
- How old are they?
- One's 34, and the other one's 30.
- You're quite close in age.
- How tall are they?
- They're both 183 cm, like me.
- Do you see them very often?
- I see them once a week.

词汇锚点：
- middle / lucky / close / tall / both / once
- close in age / close in height
- once a week / once a month / once a year

语法锚点：
- How + 形容词 + be + 主语
- How old / How tall / How big / How long
- be 动词需和后面的主语保持一致

现实语境扩展：
- 介绍兄弟姐妹数量、排行、年龄、身高。
- 描述家人是否住得近、多久见一次。
- 对比年龄、身高、居住距离。

### B. 描述人物：外貌、性格与职业

对应主题：2. My new boyfriend

核心表达：
- Who is that guy on your phone?
- That's my new boyfriend.
- He's so handsome.
- What is he like?
- He is a very kind man.
- He is pretty smart.
- Does he have a good job?
- He's a doctor.
- He loves dogs too.

词汇锚点：
- guy / boyfriend / handsome / kind / smart / job / lucky

语法锚点：
- 形容词 + 名词
- a kind man / a handsome doctor / a beautiful place / a busy street
- 形容词也可放在 be 动词后描述主语

现实语境扩展：
- 根据照片介绍朋友、伴侣、同事。
- 描述一个人的性格、外貌、职业和兴趣。
- 区分“他长什么样”和“他是什么样的人”的问法。

### C. 最喜欢的城市与特定事物

对应主题：3. What's your favorite city?

核心表达：
- What's your favorite city?
- I love going to the local cafes.
- I've never been there before.
- What's it like?
- The museums are great.
- The bars and restaurants are cool.
- The people are awesome.
- What's the weather like?
- It's too hot in the summer.

词汇锚点：
- favorite / local / famous / museums / bars / awesome / amazing

语法锚点：
- the + 名词 表示特定的人或物
- the 后可以接单数或复数名词
- 姓名、城市名、品牌名等通常不加 the

现实语境扩展：
- 介绍最喜欢的城市、当地餐馆、博物馆、天气和人。
- 对第一次听说的城市使用 What's it like?
- 对比不同季节的城市体验。

### D. 作息与时间介词

对应主题：4. When do you go to bed?

核心表达：
- When do you go to bed?
- I go to bed at 10:00 on weekdays.
- I go to bed at 11:00 on weekends.
- I go to bed at 1:00 every night.
- Why do you stay up so late?
- Sleep is really important.

词汇锚点：
- go to bed / weekdays / early / every / stay up / late / healthy / important

语法锚点：
- at + 具体时刻：at 10:00 / at noon / at night
- on + 某一天或具体日期：on Sunday / on Monday morning / on May the 4th
- every + 时间单位 表示重复习惯

现实语境扩展：
- 描述工作日与周末不同作息。
- 询问熬夜原因、起床时间、睡眠习惯。
- 将时间表达嵌入日常安排。

### E. 早餐、频率副词与健康饮食

对应主题：5. Do you eat breakfast?

核心表达：
- Do you often eat breakfast?
- I always eat breakfast.
- I want to lose weight.
- I should eat less food.
- I sometimes have a salad.
- That's good for me, right?
- It's not enough.
- Can you give me some healthy breakfast ideas?

词汇锚点：
- important / meal / lose weight / less / good for / enough / hungry / healthy

语法锚点：
- 频率副词：always / usually / often / sometimes / never
- 教材给出的频率顺序：always > usually > often > sometimes > never
- 频率副词常用于一般现在时，描述经常性动作和习惯

现实语境扩展：
- 描述早餐习惯、咖啡习惯和饮食频率。
- 对比 always / usually / often / sometimes / never。
- 用 enough / good for / hungry 描述饮食状态。

### F. How often 与频率表达

对应主题：6. How often do you visit your parents?

核心表达：
- How often do you visit your parents?
- I go home twice a year.
- That's not very often.
- They probably miss you.
- We call each other every week.
- I see them almost every day.
- You can take care of them.
- We travel together every year.

词汇锚点：
- visit / twice / then / probably / miss / each other / almost / take care of

语法锚点：
- How often + 疑问句
- once / twice
- three times / ten times / one hundred times
- every day / every week / every year 等都可回答 how often

现实语境扩展：
- 询问多久回家、看父母、锻炼、去电影院、学英语。
- 结合 once / twice / ... times 与 every... 表达频率。
- 描述家人之间互相联系和照顾。

### G. 宠物、偏好与动名词

对应主题：7. Having a pet

核心表达：
- Do you have any pets?
- I have two cats named Lady and Gaga.
- I have a dog.
- I love taking care of him.
- You love walking your dog every day?
- I also like talking to other dog owners.
- I prefer cats.
- They are independent.
- You just need to feed them.
- They don't like going outside.

词汇锚点：
- taking care / walking / exercise / owners / prefer / independent / feed / inside / outside

语法锚点：
- 主语 + like/love + doing something
- 主语 + hate/don't like + doing something
- 第三人称单数否定：doesn't like + doing

现实语境扩展：
- 介绍宠物种类、名字、日常照顾方式。
- 比较喜欢猫还是狗，并说明原因。
- 用 like/love/hate + doing 表达日常偏好。

### H. 减重、建议与 want to

对应主题：8. I want to lose weight

核心表达：
- I put on some weight over the winter.
- I want to lose weight before summer.
- What should I do?
- You need to stop eating junk food and start exercising.
- You should stop eating fast food too.
- Going to sleep early can help.
- It helps keep you healthy as well.

词汇锚点：
- put on some weight / guess / lose weight / junk food / exercising / vegetables / fast food / healthy

语法锚点：
- want to + 动词原形
- I want to go swimming.
- Does he want to go for a walk?
- They want to have a party.
- stop/start + doing 作为本课对话中的高频搭配继续积累

现实语境扩展：
- 表达近期健康目标和个人计划。
- 询问 What should I do? 并给出简单建议。
- 把饮食、运动、睡眠三个话题串成一段连续对话。

## 第一阶段角色扮演建议

可把本批 8 个主题串成几个连续场景：
1. 认识新朋友后聊家庭成员、年龄、身高和见面频率。
2. 看手机照片后描述朋友或伴侣的性格、外貌和职业。
3. 讨论最喜欢的城市、当地景点、餐馆和天气。
4. 聊工作日与周末的睡眠时间、早餐习惯和饮食频率。
5. 询问多久看父母、多久锻炼，把 how often 和频率副词结合使用。
6. 谈宠物偏好，再自然过渡到 exercise、healthy、lose weight。
7. 以“我想减重”为结尾，练习 want to / should / need to / stop doing / start doing。

## 2026-10-03 继续新增：暑假计划、时间表达、航班、旧照片、约会与分手

本次继续识别并纳入 6 个 A2 主题：
9. My summer vacation
10. Waiting for the girlfriend
11. When is your flight?
12. An old photo
13. How did the date go?
14. Talking about a breakup

### I. 暑假、出行计划与 how long

对应主题：9. My summer vacation

核心表达：
- How long is your summer vacation this year?
- My vacation is only two weeks.
- I need to return to work.
- Are you going to take a trip during that time?
- I'll just stay home.
- Maybe I will take a dance class.
- I'm going abroad for three months.
- I should go back to school.
- My parents are paying for the flight.

词汇锚点：
- summer vacation / return / trip / during / home / abroad / go back / flight

语法锚点：
- How long + be + ...?
- 用于询问一段时间的长度
- three days / two weeks / one year 等回答时长
- How long was ...? / How long did ...? 可用于过去时

现实语境扩展：
- 讨论假期有多长、是否旅行、是否待在家里。
- 表达出国、回学校、回工作岗位等计划。
- 用 how long 询问会议、航班、等待时间等。

### J. 约会迟到与 quarter / half 时间表达

对应主题：10. Waiting for the girlfriend

核心表达：
- Are you on your way?
- It's half past seven.
- The movie is starting soon.
- The movie starts at a quarter past eight, right?
- No, it starts at a quarter to eight.
- I need to get ready.
- I just need to put on some makeup.

词汇锚点：
- on your way / half / a quarter / get ready / put on / makeup

语法锚点：
- half past + 小时
- a quarter past + 小时
- a quarter to + 小时
- past 表示整点之后，to 表示距离下一个整点还有多少分钟
- half 只和 past 搭配

现实语境扩展：
- 约电影、吃饭、会议时确认具体时间。
- 对方记错时间时进行纠正。
- 描述出门前正在准备什么。

### K. 航班、堵车与 past / to 复习

对应主题：11. When is your flight?

核心表达：
- Could you drive a bit faster?
- I'm a little late.
- When is your flight?
- It takes off at five to four.
- We are moving so slowly.
- There is a lot of traffic today.
- Do you know another route?
- I hope we can get there by ten past three.
- It might be faster.
- Fingers crossed!

词汇锚点：
- driver / faster / early / slowly / traffic / route / hope / fingers crossed

语法锚点：
- 直接读时间：two thirty-five
- 分钟 + past + 小时
- 分钟 + to + 下一个小时
- 7:50 可读 seven fifty，也可读 ten to eight

现实语境扩展：
- 打车去机场时说明航班时间和迟到风险。
- 请求司机加快速度或换路线。
- 用 hope / might / faster 表达希望和可能性。

### L. 旧照片、过去时间与 ago / back then / that

对应主题：12. An old photo

核心表达：
- Who are these two guys with you in this old photo?
- Those are my old college friends.
- The one on the right is Billy.
- The one on the left is Sam.
- When did you take this photo?
- About fifteen years ago.
- We were on a road trip back then.
- We drove across the USA.
- Sounds exciting!

词汇锚点：
- guys / college / road trip / everywhere / across / sounds / exciting

语法锚点：
- 时间 + ago：three years ago
- back then：那时
- that + 过去时间：that summer / that day
- last + 时间：last month / last December

现实语境扩展：
- 看老照片并询问人物、地点和拍摄时间。
- 回忆过去的旅行和朋友。
- 用 ago / back then / that day / last ... 组织过去叙事。

### M. 约会复盘与 be 动词过去式

对应主题：13. How did the date go?

核心表达：
- How did the date go last night?
- It was an alright date.
- We didn't have much in common.
- We ran out of things to say.
- It was just boring.
- We were both happy with the food.
- He was very sweet.
- Did he text you after?
- I don't think we'll go out again.

词汇锚点：
- how did the date go / in common / ran out of / boring / happy with / sweet / go out

语法锚点：
- am / is -> was
- are -> were
- wasn't / weren't
- 过去时中 be 动词仍需主谓一致

现实语境扩展：
- 回顾一次约会、聚餐或活动进行得怎么样。
- 描述是否有共同话题、是否无聊、对食物或服务是否满意。
- 用 was/were 评价过去的人、事、地点和状态。

### N. 分手、过去时疑问词与人物评价

对应主题：14. Talking about a breakup

核心表达：
- Where was your boyfriend today?
- We ended our relationship a few weeks ago.
- Why did you two break up?
- He was really messy.
- He was a little cheap too.
- It wasn't a very happy relationship.
- Are you looking for a new boyfriend?
- I love being single.

词汇锚点：
- ended / relationship / break up / handsome / messy / cheap / worst / single

语法锚点：
- 疑问词 + was/were + 主语
- 疑问词 + did + 主语 + 动词原形
- Where was ...? / Why were ...?
- Where did you go? / When did she come back?

现实语境扩展：
- 询问过去发生了什么、为什么发生。
- 描述一段关系结束的原因和对人物的评价。
- 把 was/were 与 did 提问形式放在同一对话中练习。

## 第二阶段角色扮演建议

可把本批 6 个主题串成几个连续场景：
1. 先聊暑假有多长、是否旅行、是否出国，练习 how long。
2. 约电影却记错时间，再练 half / quarter / past / to。
3. 接着模拟赶飞机，加入堵车、换路线、航班起飞时间。
4. 到旅行地后看到旧照片，回忆 many years ago / back then / that summer。
5. 再进入社交话题：复盘一次约会，练 was/were 与一般过去时。
6. 最后讨论一段关系为什么结束，集中练习 where/why/when + was/were/did。
