# 开言 B1 语境练习计划

日期：2026-10-03
状态：学习中

## 训练原则

- 以用户上传的 B1 课后讲义为依据。
- 重点不是背原句，而是把词汇、语法和场景迁移到真实口语。
- ChatGPT 在对话中扮演真人角色，优先继续交流；一轮结束后再集中纠错。
- 只有在脱离提示、换场景后仍能独立表达，才考虑标记为“已学会”。

## 2026-10-03 首批新增：购物、退换、议价、送礼与会员

本批纳入 7 个 B1 主题：

3. Exchange and refund
4. Can you give me a better deal?
5. Buying flowers for your girlfriend
6. Choosing a birthday cake
7. Getting a membership card

### 1. Buying sports shoes

场景：
- 运动鞋店选鞋
- 说明用途、鞋型、尺码
- 试穿前确认库存

核心表达：
- I'm looking for a pair of sports shoes.
- Preferably a pair of sneakers I can wear at the gym and outside.
- Would you like high-tops or low-tops?
- Let's go with low-tops.
- Those look comfortable, but not quite what I'm looking for.
- Do you have them in a size 10?
- I'll go grab a pair from the back so you can try them on.

词汇锚点：
- particular / preferably / sneakers
- high-tops / low-tops
- comfortable / cross-trainers
- all-around shoes / have them in / grab

语法锚点：
- may 表示礼貌请求与可能性
- May I help you...?
- We may go there after lunch.
- May I ask...?

口语迁移：
- 说明自己想买什么、偏好什么、是否合适、有没有尺码。
- 用 not quite what I'm looking for 委婉表达“不太符合要求”。

### 2. Shopping in a gift store

场景：
- 给家人、亲戚、伴侣挑纪念品
- 请求店员推荐
- 比较不同礼物

核心表达：
- I'm looking to buy some souvenirs for a few family members.
- I'd like to give them something a bit more special.
- What do you suggest for the older tourist?
- I suppose my younger cousin might like one.
- Last but not least, I need something for my girlfriend.
- I'll take it!

词汇锚点：
- souvenirs / popular / special
- magnets / suppose / suggest
- poster / last but not least / tote bag

语法锚点：
- might 表示不确定的可能性
- She might do that.
- She might be doing that.
- She might have done that.
- She might not do that.

口语迁移：
- 给别人买礼物时表达“可能会喜欢”“你建议什么”。
- 练习 suggest / recommendation 与 might 的组合。

### 3. Exchange and refund

场景：
- 退货、换货、退款
- 出示收据
- 商店政策沟通

核心表达：
- I'd like to return this dress.
- Did you bring the receipt?
- Here you go.
- I can help you exchange it for another one.
- Would I be able to get a full refund?
- We have a no-refund policy.
- If I were you, I would change that policy.

词汇锚点：
- return / sleeves / receipt
- credit / exchange / full refund
- policy / take clothes back

语法锚点：
- If I were you, I would...
- If I were you, I wouldn't...
- 用于虚拟情景下给建议

口语迁移：
- 退货时说明原因。
- 询问能否退款、换货、拿 store credit。
- 用 If I were you... 给建议。

### 4. Can you give me a better deal?

场景：
- 议价、比较价格和质量
- 询问折扣
- 达成交易

核心表达：
- These dresses really caught my eye.
- The shop next door charges much less.
- I'm not sure these are worth the price.
- Maybe if there were a discount...
- At this price, it's a real bargain.
- Can you give me a better deal?
- You've got a deal!

词汇锚点：
- catch/caught my eye
- charge / quality / cheap
- worth / discount / silk
- pattern / bargain / deal
- I'll tell you what / include

语法锚点：
- be + (not) the same + noun + as...
- not the same quality as...
- not the same color as...
- not the same price as...

口语迁移：
- 比较两家店价格、质量、颜色、尺寸。
- 练习 worth the price / better deal / bargain。

### 5. Buying flowers for your girlfriend

场景：
- 在花店挑花
- 描述对方喜好
- 包装服务

核心表达：
- I need to buy flowers for my girlfriend.
- I don't have a clue what she'll like.
- A bouquet of red roses is a classic choice.
- I'd like to give her something more special.
- They look amazing!
- Do they last for a long time?
- Would you like to have them wrapped?

词汇锚点：
- don't have a clue / bouquet
- roses / classic / modern / romantic
- lilies / forget-me-nots
- last / wrapped

语法锚点：
- have/get + noun + past participle
- have them wrapped
- get my hair cut
- have my phone fixed

口语迁移：
- 描述“让别人替我做某事”。
- 用 have/get + 宾语 + 过去分词谈理发、修手机、包装礼物。

### 6. Choosing a birthday cake

场景：
- 选生日蛋糕
- 口味、尺寸、祝福语
- 临时改变决定

核心表达：
- I'm looking for a birthday cake.
- What type of cake are you thinking?
- What else do you recommend?
- Is it really rich?
- It's very light.
- I'll take the 12-inch.
- On second thought, I'll just buy it now.

词汇锚点：
- icing / recommend / mousse
- cheesecake / rich / light
- message / order / on second thought

语法锚点：
- 数字 + 单位构成复合形容词
- a 12-inch cake
- a 75-inch TV
- a three-day weekend
- a 2-liter bottle

口语迁移：
- 描述尺寸、容量、时长。
- 练习 on second thought 表达改变主意。

### 7. Getting a membership card

场景：
- 结账时办理会员
- 积分、折扣、年费
- 填写信息

核心表达：
- Do you have a membership card with us?
- If you sign up, you'll get special discounts.
- You can earn points with the card.
- It would be nice to save on groceries.
- Is there an annual fee?
- You'll get an extra 10% off your purchase.
- Sign me up!
- Fill in your info here.

词汇锚点：
- checkout / membership card / sign up
- discounts / earn / save on
- annual fee / off / purchase / fill in

语法锚点：
- first conditional
- If + subject + verb, subject + will/can/should + verb
- If you sign up, you'll get special discounts.
- You'll get special discounts if you sign up.

口语迁移：
- 商店促销、会员注册、积分与折扣。
- 练习现实条件下“如果……就……”的表达。

### 8. Fine dining

核心场景：
- 高级餐厅入座
- 询问招牌菜、特色菜
- 看菜单后点餐

核心表达：
- This is our first time here. Do you have any recommendations?
- Our signature dish is the rib-eye steak with mashed potatoes.
- Do you have any specials this evening?
- Tonight our special is a lovely five-course Maine lobster dinner.
- We'll still have a look at the menu first.
- Take your time.
- You read my mind.
- We're ready to order.

词汇锚点：
- server / recommendations / certainly
- signature dish / rib-eye steak / mashed potatoes
- specials / course / lobster
- fine dining / grilled salmon

语法锚点：
- 复合形容词 compound adjectives
- five-course dinner
- full-time job
- well-made table
- 500-page book
- six-year-old girl

口语迁移：
- 模拟高档餐厅，由服务员主动介绍招牌菜、套餐和今日特色。
- 用户需要询问推荐、比较菜品、延迟决定并最终点餐。

### 9. Red wine or white wine?

核心场景：
- 看酒单
- 红白葡萄酒选择
- 食物与酒水搭配

核心表达：
- This is quite the wine list.
- I'm not sure about what to eat yet.
- Cab's rich flavor pairs well with red meats.
- Generally speaking, a refreshing white pairs well with seafood and chicken.
- What if we had some oysters and a couple glasses of Muscadet first?
- That sounds like a great way to get ready for the main course.

词汇锚点：
- wine list / I'll say / plus
- Cabernet / rich / pair
- generally / refreshing / seafood
- oysters / Muscadet / main course

语法锚点：
- wh- question word + infinitive
- what to eat
- what to say
- who to call
- where to go

口语迁移：
- 餐厅里根据食物推荐饮料或酒水。
- 扩展到日常“我不知道该做什么/去哪/问谁”的表达。

### 10. Happy hour

核心场景：
- 下班后去酒吧/餐厅
- 询问优惠
- 点小吃和饮料

核心表达：
- This place looks like a great happy hour spot.
- This place is my number one after-work hangout.
- The two-for-one drink deal can't be beat.
- All appetizers are half off.
- Let's get an order of wings and an order of sliders.
- A round of beers will go great with those.
- I'll try one of their cocktails instead.

词汇锚点：
- happy hour / spot / hangout
- deal / food specials / appetizers
- half off / an order of
- sliders / a round of beers / cocktails / bartender

语法与表达锚点：
- go with = 搭配、相配
- This goes well with that.
- These shoes don't go with that jacket.
- Ice cream goes with coffee.

口语迁移：
- 比较食品、饮料、颜色、服装是否搭配。
- 练习优惠表达：two-for-one / half off / deal。

### 11. Lining up for restaurants

核心场景：
- 餐厅排队过久
- 向经理投诉
- 服务补救和道歉

核心表达：
- I'd like to make a complaint.
- What seems to be the problem?
- I have been waiting in line for a table for almost an hour.
- I'm afraid we are usually quite busy on Fridays.
- That doesn't excuse the attitude of the girl at the front door.
- I'll remind her to be more professional.
- How much longer will we have to line up?
- No charge.
- That's acceptable.
- Please accept my deepest apologies.

词汇锚点：
- complaint / waiting in line / I'm afraid
- excuse / attitude / remind / professional
- line up / charge / acceptable / apologies

语法锚点：
- Present perfect continuous
- Subject + have/has been + verb-ing
- I have been waiting for almost an hour.
- I have been waiting since 6:00.
- We've been working for nearly 10 hours.

口语迁移：
- 排队、客服、酒店、维修等待等投诉场景。
- 区分 for + 一段时间 / since + 起始时间。
- 练习礼貌投诉而不是直接发火。

### 12. Catching up with an old friend

核心场景：
- 偶遇多年未见的朋友
- 聊近况、工作、家庭和变化
- 约下次见面

核心表达：
- I hardly recognized you!
- I've been working out a bit more these days.
- How's everything going with you?
- We went through some tough times at work last year.
- Sorry to hear that!
- We're having a baby!
- I've got to run.
- We should grab a coffee soon and catch up.
- We can set up a date.

词汇锚点：
- hardly / recognize / these days
- go through / tough / horrible
- having a baby / congratulations
- grab a coffee / catch up / set up

语法锚点：
- 现在完成进行时用于描述“最近新出现、一直持续到现在”的活动
- Subject + have/has been + verb-ing
- recently / lately / these days 常与该时态搭配
- I've been working a lot lately.
- She's been traveling a lot recently.

口语迁移：
- ChatGPT 扮演多年未见的旧同事或同学。
- 用户需要自然聊工作变化、学习、健康、家庭、最近在做什么。
- 重点不是回答固定问题，而是练 catch up 式连续追问。

### 13. How was your trip?

核心场景：
- 朋友到达后聊旅途
- 航班延误、错过转机、机场过夜
- 说到目前为止的旅行感受

核心表达：
- You made it!
- My first flight was delayed three hours.
- That stinks!
- The delay caused me to miss my connecting flight.
- I had to stay at the airport hotel overnight.
- That's exactly what happened to me.
- So far it's been great.
- I can't wait to get out and see more of the sights.

词汇锚点：
- made it / horrible / delayed / stinks
- connecting flight / once / overnight
- exactly / anyway / finally
- so far / can't wait

语法锚点：
- cause + someone/something + to + verb
- Traffic caused me to be late.
- The rain caused us to cancel our plans.
- The delay caused me to miss my connecting flight.

口语迁移：
- 模拟航班、火车、长途旅行中的意外。
- 练习“原因 → 结果”的表达，而不是只用 because。
- 用 so far 描述旅程到目前为止的体验。

### 14. Planning a family activity

核心场景：
- 家庭成员协调周末活动
- 钓鱼、徒步、保龄球、看电影
- 调整并保留各自安排

核心表达：
- Grandpa invited us to go fishing this weekend.
- I'll have to confirm with your father first.
- Does he already have something in mind?
- I was hoping to take the whole family bowling.
- How about we see a movie instead?
- That way, I can go fishing and dad can keep his plans.
- Sounds like it's going to be a fun-filled weekend!

词汇锚点：
- fishing / confirm / in mind / free
- anyway / whole / bowling
- instead / keep his plans / fun-filled

语法锚点：
- I was hoping... 用过去时软化现在的请求或愿望
- I was hoping to...
- I was hoping we could...
- I was wondering...
- I was thinking...

口语迁移：
- 安排周末、旅行、家庭聚会。
- 用户需要在多人时间冲突中提出替代方案。
- 训练更委婉的表达，而不是只用 I want。

### 15. Making new friends

核心场景：
- 搬到新地方后很难交朋友
- 同事社交、兴趣聚会、报名与直接参加
- 对“别人会不会喜欢我”的担忧

核心表达：
- I've been here for a year and I still haven't made any friends.
- At work I'm actually really shy.
- You could try having more conversations with your coworkers.
- I never know what to say.
- You can meet people who share common interests with you.
- Do I need to sign up to participate?
- You could just show up.
- Just go and be yourself.

词汇锚点：
- social / shy / conversations
- include / common interests
- sign up / participate / informal
- events / show up / be yourself

语法锚点：
- 现在完成时表达持续到现在的状态
- for + 一段时间
- since + 具体起点
- I have been here for a year.
- She has been working here since 1999.

口语迁移：
- 新公司、新城市、兴趣小组、语言交换等结识新朋友场景。
- 练习描述自己社交状态和持续时间。
- 练习给别人社交建议。

### 16. Social distancing

核心场景：
- 讨论长期在家、日常习惯变化
- 社交距离、口罩、必要措施
- 讨论某事“正在被处理”

核心表达：
- I can't remember the last time I was stuck at home this long.
- Day in, day out...
- Do you think this will be the new normal?
- The way things are going...
- The pandemic isn't being handled too well.
- People aren't taking the necessary steps.
- It felt unnatural at first.
- Now it's almost become second nature.

词汇锚点：
- stuck / day in, day out / at-home
- social distancing / new normal
- pandemic / handled / necessary
- unnatural / shaking hands / second nature

语法锚点：
- 现在进行时 + 被动语态
- Subject + be + being + past participle
- My phone is being fixed.
- The work isn't being done fast enough.
- Masks are being worn on the subway.

口语迁移：
- 不局限于疫情，可扩展到公司项目、维修、道路施工、订单处理等。
- 练习“事情正在被处理”，且不强调执行者。

### 17. Just a minute!

核心场景：
- 出门参加派对前催促
- 化妆、换衣服、找搭配鞋
- 临时发现自己穿错场合

核心表达：
- Are you ready to roll?
- We're supposed to leave in 15 minutes.
- Just a minute.
- You've been taking forever!
- Can you hurry up?
- Give me a sec!
- I need to find matching shoes.
- I was born ready!
- I need to get changed then.

词汇锚点：
- ready to roll / just a minute
- taking forever / put on / makeup
- hurry up / give me a sec
- matching / gorgeous / I was born ready

语法锚点：
- be supposed to
- 表示义务、安排或别人对我们的预期
- We're supposed to leave in 15 minutes.
- I'm supposed to start work at 8.
- You're not supposed to wear casual clothing here.
- You were supposed to be here an hour ago.

口语迁移：
- 出门、会议、上班、赶车、参加正式活动。
- 练习催促、解释为什么还没准备好，以及“本来应该……”的过去义务。

### 18. A grocery shopping list

核心场景：
- 列家庭购物清单
- 判断什么用完了、快坏了
- 决定买多少、是否打折才买

核心表达：
- I'm making a grocery list.
- We're out of milk and eggs.
- These strawberries are about to go bad.
- Only get a pound this time.
- I'll pick up one of their roast chickens.
- Only buy it if it's on sale.
- I'm tired of eating those potato chips.
- Let's get something healthy.

词汇锚点：
- grocery list / out of / strawberries
- go bad / pound / pick up
- roast / on sale / tired of
- snacks / bedtime / popcorn

语法锚点：
- only 作副词表示限制
- only + verb
- be + only
- This store only sells cell phones.
- She was only 17.
